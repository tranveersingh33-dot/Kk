Har line mein ek bracket group likhein. Bracket se pehle ke numbers count hote hain.

Example: 23,45,67(10wp = 3 × 20 = 60.

Calculation Rules

  1. Bracket se pehle ke sabhi numbers count honge.
  2. Count × bracket value = points.
  3. Bracket ke baad wp ho to value double.
  4. Single aur double brackets supported.
  5. Sabhi groups ka total Grand Total.

Examples

23,45,67(10 = 3 × 10 = 30 23,45,67(10wp = 3 × 20 = 60 23,45,67(10)wp = 3 × 20 = 60 12,34(5)wp = 2 × 10 = 20
