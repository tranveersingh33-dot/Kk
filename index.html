Help

Har bracket group calculate hota hai. Bracket se pehle ke numbers count hote hain. Bracket ke baad WP ho to value double. Ek line mein multiple brackets bhi supported hain.

23,45,67(10 = 3 × 10 = 30 23,45,67(10wp = 3 × 20 = 60 23,45(10) 67(5) = 2 × 10 + 1 × 5 = 25 12,34(5)wp = 2 × 10 = 20

Calculation Rules

  1. Bracket se pehle ke numbers count honge.
  2. Count × bracket value = points.
  3. Bracket ke paas WP likha ho to value double.
  4. WP MODE se sabhi groups double kar sakte hain.
  5. Ek line mein multiple bracket groups supported.

Examples

23,45,67(10 = 3 × 10 = 30 23,45,67(10wp = 3 × 20 = 60 23,45,67(10)wp = 3 × 20 = 60 12,34(5)wp = 2 × 10 = 20
